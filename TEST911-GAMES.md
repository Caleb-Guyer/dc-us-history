# Short 3D Chapter 9 and 11 campaigns · 7.2.0

[Chapter 9: The Price of Progress](chapter9.html) · [Chapter 11: Across the Divide](chapter11.html) · [Test review](test911.html)

Each campaign takes about 3–5 minutes, plus time spent considering decisions and final recall. Three action missions last at most 36 active seconds each. The clock pauses for two historical decisions per mission. The games now use real 3D environments, third-person cameras, animated characters, lighting and physical scenery. Thirty authored cinematic shots use close-ups, tracking moves, crane moves and map-table inserts. Scenes advance automatically or can be skipped; no timer applies to the six final review questions.

| Campaign | Mission | What the action teaches |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Lowell mill, 1824 | Work repeated loom tasks and build clean timing chains; connect specialization to mechanization, deskilling and employer control |
| 9 | Erie Canal, 1825 | Steer a horse-towed shipment and use short surges through safe gaps; connect lower freight costs to western farms and eastern markets |
| 9 | Lowell worker petition, 1834 | Vault carts and carry petition copies; connect worker organizing to wages, the labor theory of value and unequal industrial rewards |
| 11 | Corps of Discovery, 1805 | Navigate a canoe around rocks and use paddle surges; connect scientific records and Indigenous assistance to the expedition’s route |
| 11 | San Jacinto dispatch, 1836 | Read volley markers, dash or take cover, and carry dispatches; distinguish the Alamo, San Jacinto, independence and later annexation |
| 11 | Washington newspaper delivery, 1850 | Vault street hazards and deliver the printed terms; connect the Mexican Cession, Gold Rush, Free Soil and the unresolved slavery crisis |

Use **A/D or ←/→ to steer left and right**, and **W/S or ↑/↓ to move forward and back**. These directions follow the third-person camera.

| Mission | Space or E | Shift or C |
|---|---|---|
| Factory | Hold Work near a spool and its station | — |
| Canal and river | Tap Boost, then release and let it recharge | — |
| Petition and newspaper couriers | Tap Vault to clear a cart | Tap Sprint through a clear gap |
| San Jacinto | Tap Dash past a volley | Hold Cover while a volley passes |

The visible movement and action buttons support touch. Close passes, clean vaults and well-timed factory work add score, but historical decisions still matter. Press **P or Esc** to pause. Losing a mission allows an immediate retry without losing previous checkpoints. Leaving the page retains completed missions; an unfinished action attempt restarts.

Gold objects mark objectives. Collect from at least two stations or paths to complete a mission; waiting in one place cannot finish it. Red obstacles cost condition. Factory spools require work near their station; merely touching them is insufficient. Other missions collect cargo, records or dispatches by reaching them. The simplified courses and rapid deliveries are arcade representations, not literal reconstructions. There are no rewards for killing people or seizing land.

The 3D settings distinguish the Erie towpath from the expedition’s river crossing, the Alamo from San Jacinto’s coastal prairie, the Gold Rush camp from Washington, and the 1850 capital from its later appearance. Map inserts distinguish industrial connections, transport routes, the expedition, the Missouri agreement, Texas and the Mexican Cession. The expedition route includes overland travel; it is not presented as a continuous inland waterway.

The historical montage uses a different representative participant in each mission. All dialogue is invented, clearly labeled representative speech. Lewis and Clark, Sacagawea, Houston and Santa Anna are discussed without fabricated quotations. The action does not change historical outcomes or imply one fictional courier caused a real victory. Source notes appear in each campaign menu. [The source guide](TEST911-SOURCES.md) records the chapter coverage and factual corrections used by the full test review.

The game highlights important connections across all nine Chapter 9 and 11 sections, with selected canonical questions at the end. It does not replace the complete 210-question bank or 45 bold-term cards. Game checkpoints and final recall use a separate campaign record. They never grant automatic question or flashcard credit in the review hub. The hub displays 0–3 mission completions for each campaign. Existing Chapter 9 and 11 checkpoints and best scores remain available after the 3D update; completed missions can be replayed with the new action. Earlier Chapter 10, midterm and test-review records remain separate.

Sound starts off unless a player previously enabled it. Sound on enables an original Web Audio score, synthesized effects and device speech for cinematic subtitles where supported. Nothing requires a paid service or API key. Voice availability depends on the browser/device; subtitles always remain visible. Quiet QA uses `?muted=1&preview=1`, which starts muted and uses separate campaign records.

Use **Pause scene / Resume scene** to hold a cinematic and read its subtitle. **Next shot** advances one beat; **Skip scene** goes straight to the next briefing or result.
