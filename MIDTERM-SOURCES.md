## Chapter 4

The supplied *OS Ch 4 Rule Britannia (1).docx* supports the imperial-war, Pennsylvania, Bacon's Rebellion, salutary neglect, Albany Plan, Pitt, Quebec and postwar-debt review. The named midterm priorities are King Philip's War and Hobbes/Locke. Their primary texts help avoid simplistic claims about human nature:

- [Hobbes, Leviathan](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3207/pg3207-images.html), chapters XIII and XVII–XVIII: an undivided sovereign can be an assembly as well as a monarch; institution involves a covenant.
- [Locke, Second Treatise](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/7370/7370-h/7370-h.htm): natural rights, consent, limited authority and resistance when the trust is destroyed.
- [NPS, King Philip's War](https://www.nps.gov/rowi/learn/historyculture/kingphilip.htm): Indigenous sovereignty and colonial expansion; no ambiguous question about the northern war's end date.

## Chapter 7

Based on *OS Ch 7 Creating Republican Governments.docx*, handwritten lecture notes and the supplied printed slides. Source paragraph numbers refer to the local extracted edited text, not universal textbook numbering.

- [Articles of Confederation, actual text](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/articles-of-confederation): adopted 1777, fully ratified 1781; nine-state approval for specified major matters, unanimity for amendments. Congress could borrow and issue bills of credit; its weakness was not a total inability to borrow.
- [Constitution, actual text](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript) and [Bill of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript): powers, checks and rights. The Tenth reserves undelegated powers to states **or the people**. The three-fifths calculation concerned representation/direct taxes, not voting rights. The 1808 clause concerned international slave importation, not abolition.
- [Senate, Great Compromise](https://www.senate.gov/about/origins-foundations/senate-and-constitution/great-compromise.htm): Sherman and Ellsworth, July 16, 1787. Signing September 17 was separate from state ratification.
- [National Archives, convention and ratification](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/more-perfect-union).
- [Library of Congress, Mercy Otis Warren's History](https://wwws.loc.gov/exhibits/thomas-jeffersons-library/interactives/history-of-the-american-revolution/): published 1805, correcting the handout's 1812 date.

The Convention is a coalition strategy abstraction. Scores, debate rounds and dramatized dialogue are invented. Players select arguments to gather support; the historical Constitution and its exclusions are fixed. Ratification actions are milestones, not a reenactment of each state's vote.

Washington was officially nonpartisan. Hamilton's proposed manufacturing program was not enacted in every detail. Virginia/Kentucky constitutional claims were contested. Letters of marque licensed privateering, distinct from ordinary unlicensed piracy. These qualifications are reflected in the explanations.

Hold the Harbor places an anonymous garrison trooper under Major George Armistead at Fort McHenry, September 13–14, 1814. The 25-hour bombardment is condensed. Individual supply errands, shell warnings, health recovery, barge targets and dialogue are game mechanics. Main bomb ships remain beyond effective fort-gun range; the cannon phase concerns night barges, not sinking the fleet. Key watches offshore. The flag has 15 stars and 15 stripes. Other Baltimore defenses also contributed.
