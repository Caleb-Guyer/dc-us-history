# The Reform Years · Chapter 13 games

Three short 3D vignettes reinforce the reform connections in the supplied Chapter 13. Each has a 40-second action deadline, two required historical decisions, three opening shots and two ending shots. Gameplay ends when the objectives and decisions are complete. There is no wait for the deadline after successful work.

| Story | Play | Historical connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Shared Bench | Cross between benches, time jobs, build a chain and sprint to the next task. Eight jobs across at least two benches. | Revival, millennialism, Shaker communal property and equality, transcendentalism, and different religious and secular foundations for reform. |
| The Abolition Press | Collect six pamphlet bundles across two paths, vault carts, find gaps and manage sprint energy. | Black abolitionist voices, Garrison and Douglass, moral suasion, immediatism, colonization and political abolitionism. |
| Open the Hall | Prepare six specific places as supply-carrying volunteers cross the aisles. | Women’s temperance and abolition work, teetotalism, London in 1840, Seneca Falls in 1848 and the Declaration of Sentiments. |

WASD or arrows moves. Space/E works, vaults or prepares, depending on the story. Shift/C sprints using energy, which recovers when released. In the hall, Space/E away from a task also makes a short protective dash. P/Esc pauses or resumes gameplay. Scene controls pause, advance or skip the film; captions remain visible. Touch controls provide held movement, Sprint and a primary action.

The presentation uses sculpted anatomical head geometry, clothed adult characters, jointed limbs and fingers, and physical period props, including presses, pamphlets and a declaration table. Fourteen locally bundled photographed material maps give wood, masonry, cobblestones, plaster and cloth their surface detail. Soft shadows and environment lighting ground people and props. The courier’s street recycles past her running character. Fifteen authored camera tracks include close-ups, working hands, inserts and handoffs; detailed desktop scenes add depth-aware background blur.

The Visuals button switches between Detailed and Balanced. Balanced keeps the materials and characters, using lower shadow resolution and pixel density without the cinematic lens pass. Phones start in Balanced. Scene clocks wait for the material requests to finish; a failed request retains a local procedural fallback. Reduced motion suppresses particles and sprint camera widening. No external graphics or font requests are required. See [art credits](C13-ART.md).

All participants and speech are fictional representatives. The workshop is a composite teaching space; it does not claim Shakers, Brook Farm and Owen occupied the same community. The street route and convention preparation are arcade compressions. Victory does not end slavery or immediately grant women voting rights. All fourteen bold terms appear in the scene and decision material, including contrasts in the corrective feedback. The complete [review hub](c13-review.html) retains all 104 questions and broader chapter coverage; games reinforce selected connections.

Sound starts off. Optional sound uses local synthesis and device speech, with captions as the reading path. `?muted=1&preview=1` locks sound off and uses a separate practice record. Older Chapter 13 mission IDs and the saved study record are preserved, so the update retains completed games, best scores, cards and question history.
