# Majority credits

The Chapter 10 campaign, questions, interface, dialogue and procedural environments were authored for this project. Eliza Reed and her coworkers are fictional. The game does not recreate a historical person's voice or claim that its individual errands happened.

The 14 original synthetic performances were generated locally with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache-2.0, using the MIT-licensed [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Stock voices are af_sarah, am_michael and bm_george. No voice was cloned. Recordings are packaged MP3 files; playback needs no account, live AI service or API key.

The game reuses this project's Chapter 6 home and tension scores and browser-synthesized feedback sounds. See [Chapter 6 credits](C6-CREDITS.md). Speech ducks the music. Sound can be muted; `?muted=1&preview=1` forces silence and uses a separate study record.

Rendering uses the already-bundled MIT-licensed [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) library, this project's common scene primitives and articulated Chapter 5 actors. Buildings, furnishings, the screw press, coins, merchant boats, cargo routes, wharves, shoals, parade floats, billboards and textures are drawn or generated in code. No Halo or Call of Duty assets are used.

The uploaded classroom Word document and private extraction/audit files are not published. The public [source guide](C10-SOURCES.md) identifies historical references and qualifications; [coverage](C10-COVERAGE.md) distinguishes campaign concepts from detail available in the quiz.
