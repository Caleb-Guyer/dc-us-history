# Majority credits

Release 6.2 adds the original [Chapter 10 quiz-review hub](c10-review.html), its interface and recall-card storage, and the [course section chooser](index.html). The [midterm](midterm.html) is a separate section. The campaign and hub share Chapter 10 question progress while retaining the existing midterm save namespace.

The sixteen key terms were verified against bold formatting in the uploaded article, excluding headings. Their definitions, examples and comparison notes in `c10-terms.mjs` are original study explanations grounded in that text and the existing historical audit. The flashcards and dedicated three-term/twelve-topic rehearsal reuse the original ninety-question bank; the source article is not republished. Practice records and self-rated recall do not claim a guaranteed class quiz score.

The Chapter 10 campaign, questions, interface, dialogue and procedural environments were authored for this project. Eliza Reed and her coworkers are fictional. The game does not recreate a historical person's voice or claim that its individual errands happened.

The 14 original synthetic performances were generated locally with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache-2.0, using the MIT-licensed [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Stock voices are af_sarah, am_michael and bm_george. No voice was cloned. Recordings are packaged MP3 files; playback needs no account, live AI service or API key.

The game reuses this project's Chapter 6 home and tension scores and browser-synthesized feedback sounds. See [Chapter 6 credits](C6-CREDITS.md). Speech ducks the music. Sound can be muted; `?muted=1&preview=1` forces silence and uses a separate study record.

Rendering uses the already-bundled MIT-licensed [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) library, this project's common scene primitives and articulated Chapter 5 actors. Buildings, furnishings, the screw press, coins, merchant boats, cargo routes, wharves, shoals, parade floats, billboards and textures are drawn or generated in code. No Halo or Call of Duty assets are used.

Release 6.1 adds the original `c10-cinema.mjs` scene rigs and camera tracks: character blocking, close-ups, moving props, scene-specific inserts and a restrained depiction of the later forced migration. The fourteen voiced passages retain their original recordings; short advancing subtitles replace full-paragraph captions. Players can pause, advance or skip a scene. Camera movement, lighting, street traffic, harbor wakes and rally effects are procedural rather than prerecorded video.

The action update adds crossing wagons, courier vault and close-pass rewards, selected-ship sailing boosts, timed parcel dispatch and optional rally pennants. Style scores and bonuses measure the fictional errands. They cannot change an election, avert the Panic of 1837 or prevent forced removal.

The uploaded classroom Word document and private extraction/audit files are not published. The public [source guide](C10-SOURCES.md) identifies historical references and qualifications; [coverage](C10-COVERAGE.md) distinguishes campaign concepts from detail available in the quiz.
